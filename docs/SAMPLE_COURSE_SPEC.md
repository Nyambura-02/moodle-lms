# GlobeCon Convergence Solutions LMS
## Sample Course Curriculum Specification

### 1. Course Profile
- **Course Title**: GlobeCon Fundamentals: Information Security & Operational Excellence
- **Short Name**: GC-SEC-101
- **Course Category**: Corporate Compliance & Training
- **Target Audience**: All GlobeCon Convergence Solutions employees, contractors, and onboarding personnel.
- **Estimated Duration**: 4 Hours (Self-paced)
- **Completion Criteria**: 
  1. View all required reading documents and training presentations.
  2. Watch the video walkthrough on Information Security.
  3. Achieve an 80% or higher score on the final assessment quiz.

### 2. Course Sections & Learning Activities

#### Section 0: Welcome & Orientation

- **Activities**:
  - `resource`: **GlobeCon Acceptable Use Policy (PDF Guide)**.
  - `resource`: **Cybersecurity Threat Landscape Presentation (Slide Deck)**.

- **Overview**: Regulatory standards, safeguarding customer records, and rapid incident response protocols.
- **Activities**:
  - `url` / `page`: **Video Walkthrough: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Threats** (Embedded responsive video player).

#### Section 3: Knowledge Assessment & Certification
- **Overview**: Evaluates comprehension across all modules.
- **Activities**:
  - `quiz`: **GlobeCon Security & Compliance Final Assessment** (Detailed below).
  - `feedback`: **Course Evaluation & Learner Feedback Survey**.

### 3. Assessment & Question Bank Specification

The demonstration quiz uses Moodle's native Question Bank and Quiz engine with the following question types:

| # | Question Type | Question Title & Concept | Expected Format & Grading |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1** | **Multiple Choice (Single Answer)** | Identifying Phishing Indicators | Learner identifies the primary red flag in a suspicious external email. Auto-graded (1.0 pt). |
| **Q2** | **Multiple Choice (Multiple Answers)** | Multi-Factor Authentication Practices | Learner selects all valid factors of authentication from a checklist. Auto-graded with partial points (1.0 pt). |
| **Q3** | **True / False** | Credential Sharing Policy | "It is permissible to share temporary VPN credentials with a teammate if authorized verbally." (Answer: False). Auto-graded (1.0 pt). |
| **Q4** | **Short Answer** | Least Privilege Definition | Learner enters the acronym or exact terminology for PoLP. Auto-graded with case-insensitive matching (1.0 pt). |
| **Q5** | **Essay / Extended Response** | Security Incident Escalation Procedure | Learner writes a concise 3-step action plan if ransomware is suspected on a workstation. Trainer-graded with rubric (5.0 pts). |

#### Quiz Parameters:
- **Time Limit**: 20 Minutes
- **Grade to Pass**: 80% (7.2 / 9.0 points)
- **Attempts Allowed**: 2 Attempts (Highest grade recorded)
- **Review Options**: 
  - Immediate review: Shows score only.
  - Correct answers are revealed only *after* the quiz attempt is officially closed.
